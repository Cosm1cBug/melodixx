Melodix — Feel Every Note.

A minimal, dark-first Material 3 YouTube Music client with vibrant accent colors.

• Ad-free background playback and offline downloads
• Spotify library, search and recommendations (no Premium required)
• Experimental lossless streaming (FLAC / Hi-Res) with silent fallback
• Synced, word-by-word lyrics from multiple providers
• Listen Together, music recognition, equalizer, crossfade, sleep timer
• Dynamic theming with a vibrant violet/cyan palette
• Widgets, Android Auto, Discord RPC, scrobbling, podcasts

Melodix is free software (GPL-3.0). It is not affiliated with YouTube, Google,
Spotify or Qobuz.
